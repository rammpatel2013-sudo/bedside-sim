Vent Lab

A mechanical ventilation simulator with an ICU ventilator console: live waveforms and loops, holds, P0.1 and NIF, every common mode, and a side-by-side before-and-after view.

  • 40 cases in 10 lessons, graded on what your settings do to the patient
  • 7 guided tutorials, from a screen tour to esophageal pressure
  • Free play and a scenario builder that makes shareable cases

Live bedside simulation

The two-screen bedside monitor and the student chart run on the HALO program's own server during instructor-led sessions. Your instructor sends that link at the start of a session.

Educational use only. These simulators use teaching models, not predictions for a real patient, and must not guide patient care. Source code: github.com/rammpatel2013-sudo/bedside-sim.
