Key AHA/ACC acute PE clinical categories

A
Subclinical
Incidental, asymptomatic
B
Symptomatic, low severity
PESI ≤85, sPESI 0 or Bova ≤4
B1 subsegmental
B2 segmental or larger
C
Symptomatic, elevated severity
PESI >85, sPESI ≥1 or Bova >4
C1 normal RV and biomarkers
C2 abnormal RV or ≥1 biomarker
C3 abnormal RV and ≥1 biomarker
R: SpO₂ <90%, RR ≥30 or O₂ need
D
Incipient cardiopulmonary failure
D1 transient hypotension (SBP <90 or fall >40 mm Hg, <15 min or fluid-responsive)
D2 normotensive shock (lactate >2, AKI, UO <0.5 mL/kg/h, altered mentation, CI <2.2, MAP <60, rising shock stage)
R: >6 L NC or non-rebreather
E
Cardiopulmonary failure
E1 recurrent or persistent hypotension with cardiogenic shock
E2 refractory shock (SCAI D–E) or arrest without ROSC at 30 min
R: NIV or invasive ventilation
