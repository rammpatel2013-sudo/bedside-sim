LearnBedside SimSimulator

Critical care physiology and ultrasound

Interactive chapters for residents, fellows and APPs. Heart–lung physiology comes first, following Pinsky; the ultrasound chapters show the same patient as images. Every figure is simulated and every number carries its source.

Open a chapter from its link; no account or room code needed. Chapters are teaching models set to agree with their sources, not patient data and not for clinical decisions.
